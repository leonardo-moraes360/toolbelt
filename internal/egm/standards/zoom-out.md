# Standard: zoom-out

The whole document is judged as a proposal, not as a list of requirements.

Criteria:
- Fit: the proposed solution addresses the stated problem, and the goals follow
  from that problem.
- Proportion: the size of the mechanism matches the size of the problem;
  complexity is justified by a goal.
- Boundaries: the document says what it changes and what it leaves untouched,
  and the non-goals are respected by the requirements.
- Consequences: effects on other components, users, operations and security
  are acknowledged, with the main risks named and either mitigated or accepted
  explicitly.
- Alternatives: the simplest credible alternative is discussed.
- Coherence: the document does not contradict itself or the decisions it cites.
